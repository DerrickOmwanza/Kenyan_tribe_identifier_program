# Stage 6B.8 Evidence Approval Policy

Approval applies to an evidence record, never to a person. A record may be approved only when its verified source directly supports the token-level association, the source location is precise, the statement and interpretation are conservative, evidence strength is `strong` or `very_strong`, and no unresolved contradiction requires manual review.

Approval does not require independent corroboration when a primary or otherwise adequate documentary source clearly supports the claim, but independent corroboration must never be fabricated or inferred from duplicate/derivative sources.

Shared, borrowed, adapted, and alternative associations remain explicit and non-exclusive. Unresolved evidence is retained, and weaker evidence is not rejected merely because stronger evidence exists. Dataset frequency, name position, generic names, and prefixes are descriptive or review signals only, not proof of community membership.

The approved terminology is **likely community/linguistic association based on name patterns**. An approved evidence record does not establish any individual's actual ethnicity, identity, or community membership.
