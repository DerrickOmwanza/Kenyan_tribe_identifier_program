# Stage 6B.6 Evidence Promotion Policy

## Promotion gates

Evidence may be promoted to `source_verified` only when the actual source was inspected, the source substantively supports the token-level statement, the source location is accurate, and the interpretation is conservative. A valid citation or bibliographic record alone is insufficient.

`independently_corroborated` requires the same substantive claim to be supported by a genuinely independent source. Repeated copies, search snippets, websites citing the same underlying work, and derivative dictionary entries do not establish independence. Independent source identity must be recorded in the ledger or its audit notes.

`approved` requires verified source evidence, clear token-level support, appropriate strength, conservative interpretation, preserved alternatives, and no unresolved contradiction requiring review. Approval is not a person-level classification.

## Conflicts and shared names

Conflicting evidence is retained rather than silently resolved. Shared, borrowed, adapted, inherited, and cross-community names remain separate associations with their relationship documented. In particular, Kikuyu evidence and Ekegusii borrowing/adaptation evidence for KARIUKI and NJOROGE must coexist.

## Evidence strength and reliability

Strength describes the documented evidence for the specific claim: direct token-level academic documentation may be `strong`; contextual documentation may be `moderate`; secondary or indexed leads are generally `weak`, `ambiguous`, or `needs_review` depending on what was actually inspected. Source reliability describes the source, not the certainty of an individual classification.

## Identity boundary

The ledger records likely community/linguistic associations based on documented name patterns. It must never present a person's actual ethnicity, identity, or community membership as established solely from a name. Dataset frequency, co-occurrence, and name position are not external evidence of ethnicity.
