# Stage 6B.7 Independent Corroboration Policy

Evidence is promoted only when the actual source is inspected, the token-level statement is supported, the location is accurate, and the interpretation is no broader than the source.

Independent corroboration requires a distinct publication or documented source with independent authorship and substantive support for the same token-level claim. Duplicate copies, search snippets, derivative websites, and sources that merely repeat the same Benson or prior-paper claim do not qualify.

Approval requires verified evidence, appropriate strength and reliability, conservative interpretation, preserved alternatives, and no unresolved contradiction. `source_verified` is not equivalent to `approved`.

Conflicting, shared, borrowed, and adapted associations remain separate. KARIUKI and NJOROGE must preserve both Kikuyu association and Ekegusii borrowing/adaptation evidence without an exclusive conclusion.

Evidence strength describes the documented claim, not a person's identity. Contextual academic evidence may be strong or moderate; secondary references are generally weaker. The system reports likely community/linguistic associations based on documented name patterns and never establishes actual ethnicity from a name. Dataset frequency, co-occurrence, and name position are not external evidence.
